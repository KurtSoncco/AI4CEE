Advancing Civil and Environmental Engineering Through AI

AI4CEE is a growing portfolio of course materials and case-study assignments that apply data-driven methods to civil and environmental engineering challenges.

Course Showcase

CE170A: Infrastructure Sensing and Modeling

This course examines modern sensing, analysis, and modeling techniques for critical infrastructure systems. Students learn to apply data-driven methods to understand and predict infrastructure behavior under various conditions.

About This Repository

This repository contains course materials, assignments, and interactive applications for civil and environmental engineering students. Homework 2 runs as an always-on GitHub Pages lab with precomputed FEM physics and an in-browser Gemma design critic.
