Infrastructure Sensing and Modeling

This course examines modern sensing, analysis, and modeling techniques for critical infrastructure systems. Students learn to apply data-driven methods to understand and predict infrastructure behavior under various conditions.

Assignments

Homework 1: Fukushima Nuclear Power Plant

Analyze the Fukushima Daiichi nuclear disaster using modern sensing and modeling techniques. This assignment explores how infrastructure monitoring can prevent catastrophic failures.

Topics: Structural analysis, sensor networks, risk assessment

Homework 2: I-35W Bridge Collapse

Investigate the I-35W bridge collapse in Minneapolis. Apply data-driven methods to understand the failure mechanisms and how improved monitoring could have prevented this infrastructure disaster.

Topics: Failure analysis, load modeling, inspection protocols

Course Outcomes

Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:

  • Apply sensing technologies to monitor critical infrastructure
  • Analyze infrastructure failures using data-driven methods
  • Model infrastructure behavior and predict failure modes
  • Design monitoring systems for early detection of issues
  • Communicate findings to stakeholders and decision-makers
