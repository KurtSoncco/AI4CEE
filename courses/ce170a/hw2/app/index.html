How to read this model
  • Axial members only. Truss bars carry axial force; they do not resist flexure. A strain gauge on a member reports axial microstrain along the bar axis, not bending strain.
  • Displacement sensors. At a joint, the reading is vertical nodal deflection in the deck plane. At a member midpoint, the reading is the average vertical deflection of the two end joints.
  • Accelerometers. The moving-load analysis is quasi-static. Reported acceleration is derived from the time rate of change of vertical deflection as the vehicle crosses at 20 model-units per second; it is not a dynamic impact simulation.
  • Model units. All responses are expressed in model units rather than physical meters. Compare sensors and load cases relative to one another on this truss geometry.
