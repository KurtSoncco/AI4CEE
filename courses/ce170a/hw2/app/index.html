How to read this model

  • Truss bars carry axial force only. Members do not bend; they stretch or compress along their length. A Strain Gauge on a beam reports that axial response (microstrain). It is not the bar “bending.”
  • Displacement sensors measure vertical joint motion. At a Joint, the reading is node deflection in the deck plane. At a Beam, the reading is the average vertical motion of the two end joints (midpoint proxy)—not elongation along the bar axis.
  • Accelerometers are quasi-static here. The solver is static at each load position; “acceleration” comes from how fast that vertical deflection changes as the vehicle crosses at 20 model-units/s. It is not a dynamic impact model.
  • Units are model units, not real meters. Compare sensors and load cases relative to each other on this bridge geometry.
  • Use the deflected-shape explorer below to see the undeformed truss (gray) versus the exaggerated deflected shape (blue) as the first vehicle moves along the deck.
